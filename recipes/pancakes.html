Pancakes

>

Description

Fluffy, golden pancakes that are crispy on the outside and soft on the insede. Perfect for a slow morning with maple syrup, butter, and your favorite fruits.

ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 teasspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted

Steps

  1. In a lrge bowl, mix the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt
  2. Add milk, egg, and the melted butter. Stir until just combined - a few lumps are fine
  3. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat
  4. Pour 1/4 cup of butter for each pancake and cook umtil bubbles form on the surface, about 2 minutes
  5. Flip and cook the onther side until golden, about 1 minute more
  6. Serve hot with maple syrup, butter, and fruits.

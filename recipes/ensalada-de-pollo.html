Ensalada de Pollo

Description

A fresh and creamy chicken salad, perfect for sandwiches, wraps, or served over a bed of lettuce. Quick to make and even better the next day.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded or diced
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 celery stalk, finely diced
  • 1/4 red onion, finely diced
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the chicken, mayonnaise, and Dijon mustard.
  2. Add the celery and red onion and mix well.
  3. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
  4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes so the flavors come together.
  5. Serve in sandwiches, wraps, or over lettuce.

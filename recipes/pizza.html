Pizza

Description

A homemade pizza with a crispy crust, rich tomato sauce, and melted mozzarella. Simple, fun to make, and easy to customize with your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Toppings of your choice (pepperoni, peppers, mushrooms)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the dough on a floured surface and place it on a baking sheet.
  3. Brush the dough lightly with olive oil.
  4. Spread the tomato sauce evenly, leaving a small border around the edges.
  5. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese and add your favorite toppings.
  6. Sprinkle with oregano and bake for 12–15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  7. Let it cool for a few minutes, slice, and serve.
